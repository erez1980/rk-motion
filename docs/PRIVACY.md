# RK Motion privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

RK Motion for iPhone is a local video editor. Its analysis and export run on
your device. It does not require an account and does not send your videos,
audio, activity scores or edits to a developer-operated server. The iPhone
app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

## Files and permissions

You select videos using Apple's Photos picker or the Files picker. RK Motion
copies selected media into its private storage so you can edit it again.
Media that exists only in iCloud may be downloaded by the system picker.
The app requests permission to add to Photos only when you choose to save
an exported movie. You can instead share the file through the system share
sheet; the destination you select handles the shared file under its own
policy. RK Motion does not request access to your entire Photos library.

Projects, source files, imported music and exports remain on the device until
you delete them. You can delete a project inside the app. Deleting the app
removes its local app storage. Copies you have saved to Photos, Files or other
destinations are independent and must be deleted there. System/device backups
may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

The app stores preferences locally and checks available disk space before
copying or exporting files. This information is not used for tracking.

## Support and changes

For support, use https://github.com/erez1980/rk-motion/issues. Do not attach
private videos or personal information to public issues. GitHub handles
information you choose to post according to its own privacy policy.

This policy applies to the standalone iPhone app. Changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy.
